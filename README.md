# Quote-Hub

> **A peaceful, focused corner of the web dedicated entirely to the power of words.**  
> *Think of it as your favorite social feed, but exclusively for quotes.*
---

## What is Quote-Hub?

In a fast-paced internet full of noise, arguments, and endless notifications, **Quote-Hub** offers a quiet sanctuary. It is a social platform built around a single, timeless idea: **great words deserve the spotlight.**

Whether it is a centuries-old philosophical insight, an unforgettable line from your favorite book or film, or an original thought you penned over morning coffee, Quote-Hub gives your words a clean, distraction-free stage to shine.

---

## Why Quote-Hub? (A Refreshing Social Experience)

Most social networks try to keep you hooked with loud debates, complicated algorithms, and overwhelming comment threads. Quote-Hub is intentionally different:

- **No Comment Drama:** We removed comment sections on purpose. Quotes stand on their own without spiraling into debate clubs or flame wars.
- **Quote-First Design:** Profiles and vanity metrics take a back seat. The words and ideas are always the main event.
- **Peaceful Scrolling:** Enjoy reading at your own pace without intrusive popups, noisy clutter, or unsolicited direct messages.
- **Respect for Attribution:** Celebrate both original thoughts and timeless quotes by giving authors the credit they deserve.

---

## What You Can Do

### Share Memorable Thoughts
Have an insightful phrase, a poetic thought, or a piece of wisdom? Share quotes of up to **280 characters** in just a couple of clicks.

### Give Credit Where It's Due
- **Original Thoughts:** Mark a quote as *Original* with one tap. Your display name is automatically credited as the author.
- **Historical & Attributed Quotes:** Enter the name of the person who said it (from Marcus Aurelius to Maya Angelou) so readers always know who wrote the line.
- **Anonymous Lines:** Leave the author blank if you simply want the words to speak for themselves.

### Dress Your Words with Themes
Quotes are an art form, and visual typography brings them to life. You can choose from three distinct visual styles when posting:

| Theme | Aesthetic | Best For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Classic Serif** | Warm amber tones with timeless literary serif italics | Poetry, ancient philosophy, and vintage book excerpts |
| **Modern Sans** | Clean, crisp, contemporary lines with balanced contrast | Daily motivation, modern life advice, and quick quips |
| **Minimal Editorial** | Expressive editorial type with a sleek left accent border | Bold statements, creative prose, and magazine-style excerpts |

### Choose Your Reading Mode
Everyone enjoys reading differently. With Quote-Hub's **Presentation Mode toggle**, you can switch anytime between:
1. **Card Presentation:** Beautiful stylized cards highlighting the author's chosen theme.
2. **Minimal Text Mode:** A clean, streamlined reading stream focused on pure typography and high readability.

### Your Private Inspiration Vault
Ever stumble across a line that changes your perspective? Tap the **Bookmark** icon to save any quote directly to your private library. Your saved list is 100% private—a personal pocket journal of inspiration only you can access.

### Discover & Connect
- **For You Feed:** Explore trending quotes and inspiring thoughts popular across the community.
- **Following Feed:** Follow friends, writers, and thinkers to see their latest quotes and reposts in a chronological timeline.
- **Reposts & Likes:** Tap the heart icon to show appreciation, or repost a quote to share it with your own followers.
- **Search:** Search across quotes, specific authors, or usernames in seconds.

## Getting Started in 3 Simple Steps

Getting started with Quote-Hub takes less than a minute:

1. **Create an Account:** Sign up with your email or log in instantly with your Google account.
2. **Explore the Feed:** Scroll through the **For You** tab to discover quotes that resonate with you, or search for your favorite authors.
3. **Post & Collect:** Hit **New Quote**, type out something meaningful, pick a style, and post. Save gems you love along the way!

## Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I post my own quotes?</strong></summary>
<br />
Yes! When creating a quote, simply switch on the <strong>Original</strong> toggle. Quote-Hub will proudly display your name as the author.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can other people see my saved quotes?</strong></summary>
<br />
No. Your saved quotes are completely private to your account. You can think of it as your personal digital reading notebook.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Why are there no comments?</strong></summary>
<br />
We intentionally designed Quote-Hub without comments so people can reflect on quotes without getting pulled into arguments or negativity. If a quote moves you, you can like it, save it, or repost it!
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I edit or delete a quote after posting?</strong></summary>
<br />
Yes. If you spot a typo or want to remove something you wrote, you can edit or delete your quotes at any time from your profile or the feed.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Does it work on mobile phones and tablets?</strong></summary>
<br />
Yes. Quote-Hub is fully responsive and looks great on smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktop screens.
</details>

## Want to Try Running It on Your Computer?

If you're curious to run Quote-Hub locally on your own computer, here is the simple 3-step guide:

1. **Get the files:** Download or clone this project folder.
2. **Install:** Open your computer's terminal in this folder and type:
   ```bash
   npm install
   ```
3. **Start the app:** Type:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```
   Then open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your web browser to start exploring!
